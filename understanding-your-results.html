Understanding Your Results

Overview

Adaptive Recoverability provides a structured assessment of Organisational Recoverability based on the information supplied at the time of assessment.

The greatest value comes from considering the assessment as a whole. Each output contributes to an overall understanding of Organisational Recoverability and should not be interpreted in isolation.

Recoverability Score

The Recoverability Score provides an overall indication of an organisation's capacity to withstand disruption, adapt to changing conditions and recover over time.

Higher scores generally indicate stronger Organisational Recoverability based on the information provided, while lower scores may identify structural characteristics that warrant further consideration.

Recoverability Classification

The Recoverability Classification summarises the organisation's current recoverability state, providing a practical and consistent interpretation of the overall assessment.

Environmental Comparisons

Recoverability changes as operating conditions change.

Environmental Comparisons help organisations understand how assessment outcomes may differ across alternative operating environments, providing additional context beyond a single assessment result.

Organisational Strengths

Strengths identify structural characteristics that contribute positively to Organisational Recoverability and long-term organisational resilience.

Structural Vulnerabilities

Structural Vulnerabilities identify characteristics that may reduce Organisational Recoverability or become more significant as operating conditions change.

They should be viewed as opportunities for further investigation, discussion and continuous improvement.

Priority Considerations

Priority Considerations identify areas where structural improvements may have the greatest influence on Organisational Recoverability.

These considerations are intended to support informed decision-making and should always be interpreted alongside organisational context and professional judgement.

Assessment Confidence

Assessment Confidence reflects the completeness and consistency of the information supporting the assessment and assists organisations in understanding the confidence that may reasonably be placed in the assessment results.

Professional Judgement

Adaptive Recoverability is designed to support informed organisational decision-making.

Assessment outputs should be interpreted alongside organisational context, professional expertise and other relevant operational, financial and regulatory information. They are intended to support—not replace—existing governance processes, internal expertise or professional judgement.

Using Assessment Outputs

Assessment outputs may be incorporated into existing governance, enterprise risk management and organisational resilience processes at the organisation's discretion.

Adaptive Recoverability Systems provides the assessment capability itself and does not participate in the implementation, integration or operation of customer governance frameworks or internal decision-making processes.

This assessment identifies structural recoverability considerations only. It does not constitute financial, investment, legal, regulatory, or other professional advice.
