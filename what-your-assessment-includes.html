What Your Assessment Includes

Adaptive Recoverability provides a structured assessment of Organisational Recoverability—an organisation's capacity to withstand disruption, adapt to changing conditions and recover over time.

Rather than focusing solely on individual risks or historical events, the assessment evaluates the organisational characteristics that influence recoverability across a range of operating environments. The result is an additional perspective designed to complement existing governance, enterprise risk management and organisational resilience practices.

Adaptive Recoverability supports informed decision-making by providing a structured assessment of Organisational Recoverability. Assessment outputs should be considered alongside professional judgement, organisational context and other relevant information. They do not constitute financial, legal, regulatory or other professional advice.

Assessment Outputs

Every assessment includes:

Recoverability Score

An overall measure of Organisational Recoverability based on the information provided.

Recoverability Classification

A clear summary of the organisation's current recoverability state.

Environmental Comparisons

Insights into how recoverability may change under different operating conditions.

Organisational Strengths

Structural characteristics that contribute positively to recoverability.

Structural Vulnerabilities

Characteristics that may reduce recoverability or become increasingly significant as operating conditions change.

Priority Considerations

Areas most likely to strengthen Organisational Recoverability if addressed.

Assessment Confidence

An indication of the completeness and consistency of the information supporting the assessment.

Supporting Decision-Making

Adaptive Recoverability is designed to support strategic planning, governance and organisational resilience by providing an additional perspective on Organisational Recoverability.

Assessment outputs are intended to complement existing governance frameworks rather than replace organisational expertise, internal decision-making or professional judgement.

Platform Scope

Adaptive Recoverability Systems provides licensed access to the Adaptive Recoverability reasoning engine through a hosted platform and API.

The platform delivers structured Organisational Recoverability assessments based on the information provided by the customer.

Adaptive Recoverability Systems does not provide systems integration, implementation services, workflow design, organisational change management or consulting as part of the standard platform offering. Organisations remain responsible for determining how assessment outputs are incorporated into their own governance, risk management and operational processes.

This assessment identifies structural recoverability considerations only. It does not constitute financial, investment, legal, regulatory, or other professional advice.
